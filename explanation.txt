FARMER-PALANTIR: WHAT THIS IS AND HOW IT WORKS
Written 2026-10-03 for the co-founders.

1. THE IDEA

Open data intelligence for Swedish farms, plus robots that can act on it.
Everything is open source and free to use.

There are two halves, and they share one data layer.

  a) Data intelligence for farmers. You pick a field on a map and get one
     place that shows its terrain, slope, imagery, sun and weather.

  b) Applied intelligence for automated farming. The same field data builds
     a simulator, and a robot driving model is trained and tested in that
     simulator. The model is then compressed so it runs on a small computer
     inside the tractor cab.

Sweden is a good place to start because the key data is open:
  - Lantmäteriet: 1 m elevation and 0.16-0.5 m aerial photos (CC BY 4.0).
  - SMHI: weather.
  - Jordbruksverket: field parcels.


2. THE HACKATHON MVP: FROM A MAP CLICK TO A COMPRESSED ROBOT MODEL

  Step 1  Pick a place.
          A map page in the browser. Click a field in Sweden, choose the size
          of the area (100-1000 m), and press "Build site".

  Step 2  Build the site.
          The system downloads elevation and an aerial photo for that square
          from Lantmäteriet. It falls back to Copernicus 30 m satellite data
          when no Lantmäteriet account is set up, and to a made-up demo field
          when there is no internet. It also computes a short field summary:
          elevation, slope, area and sun angle.

  Step 3  Simulate.
          A 3D driving simulator of that exact field, built on MuJoCo. A
          tractor drives crop rows over the real terrain. Rain lowers grip,
          the sun angle changes the light, and slopes slow the tractor.

  Step 4  Make demonstrations.
          A scripted "expert" driver follows the rows. Its drives are saved
          as training data in the format NVIDIA's robot model expects.

  Step 5  Train.
          NVIDIA GR00T N1.7 learns to drive from the demos. GR00T is an open
          robot foundation model with 3B parameters, and its licence allows
          commercial use. Training runs on our B200 GPU at evroc.

  Step 6  Compress.
          Several smaller versions of the trained model are made:
            - fewer denoising steps
            - lower precision numbers (FP8 or NVFP4)
            - removed layers
          Which parts can be shrunk is decided by testing the model in the
          simulator ("sim-in-the-loop"), not by generic rules. This is the
          same idea LobBot uses for language models, applied to robots.

  Step 7  Score.
          Every version drives the same simulated episodes. We measure
          success rate, how far it strays from the row, size in GB, and speed.

  Step 8  Show.
          - out/training.mp4: a short video of the field, the expert, the
            trained model and each compressed model driving.
          - out/footprint_vs_performance.png: a graph of how much smaller
            each model is against how well it still drives.


3. WHY IT MATTERS

Big AI models do not fit in a tractor. A model that is small, fast and
tested on your own field's terrain and weather is what makes autonomy cheap
enough for ordinary farms. Free and open means no farmer is locked into a
vendor.


4. HOW THE CONTROL SYSTEM IS MEANT TO WORK (AFTER THE HACKATHON)

  - The AI model only plans the path and speed.
  - A standard controller steers the tractor along that path using RTK GPS.
  - Implements (sprayer, hitch, PTO) are controlled over ISOBUS (ISO 11783),
    the industry standard.
  - A separate safety system handles obstacles, operating zones and the
    emergency stop, following ISO 18497. The AI model is never part of the
    safety system.


5. STATUS (BE HONEST WHEN DEMOING)

  Built and tested on a laptop with no GPU:
    - map picker
    - site building
    - simulator and scripted expert driver
    - video recording
    - training-data export
    - compression maths
    - full pipeline in "dry run" mode

  Not yet run for real (needs the B200):
    - GR00T training
    - GR00T compression on the real model

  DRY RUN NUMBERS ARE NOT RESULTS. In dry run the "models" are the expert
  driver with added noise. The video and graph say DRY RUN on them. Only
  show numbers from a real run on the B200.

  Needs an account:
    - Real Lantmäteriet data needs a free Geotorget account at
      geotorget.lantmateriet.se. Order the free Markhöjdmodell and Ortofoto
      products, then set LANTMATERIET_USER and LANTMATERIET_PASSWORD on the
      server.
    - GR00T's backbone is gated on Hugging Face, so the server needs a
      Hugging Face token (HF_TOKEN).

  Known limits:
    - The simulator uses simple vehicle physics, not full wheel and soil
      contact.
    - Isaac Sim (NVIDIA's photoreal simulator) cannot run on the B200
      because the B200 has no RT cores.
    - GR00T was pre-trained on robot arms and humanoids, not tractors, so it
      learns driving entirely from our demos.
    - For real farm driving after the hackathon, NVIDIA's Alpamayo driving
      model fits better, but commercial use requires asking NVIDIA.
